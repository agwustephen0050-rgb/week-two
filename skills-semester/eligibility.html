Eligibility & Registration

Join the SkillForge Semester and develop practical skills for the future.

Who Can Apply?

Registered Student

Applicant must be a registered student of Ebonyi State University of ICT, Science and Technology.

Commitment

Students must be willing to attend practical classes and complete projects.

Good Conduct

Applicants should maintain discipline and cooperate with instructors.

Important Notice

  • Complete every required field correctly.
  • Upload a recent passport photograph.
  • Select only one SkillForge programme.
  • Training begins on 20th July, 2026.
  • Join your assigned WhatsApp group after registration.
