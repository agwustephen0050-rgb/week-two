About SkillForge Semester

Practical Skills. Real Future.

What is SkillForge Semester?

SkillForge Semester is a practical training programme organised by Ebonyi State University of ICT, Science and Technology, Offerekpe. The programme is designed to equip students with industry-relevant practical skills alongside their academic studies. Through practical classes, projects and mentorship, students develop valuable knowledge that prepares them for employment and entrepreneurship.

Objectives

Skill Development

Equip students with practical skills.

Innovation

Encourage creativity and problem-solving.

Entrepreneurship

Prepare students for self-employment and industry.
