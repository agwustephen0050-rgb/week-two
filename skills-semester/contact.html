SkillForge Semester is a practical digital technology training programme designed to help students develop useful technical and practical skills.

Eligible students of the university can participate in the available SkillForge training programmes.

SkillForge offers several practical training areas, including Digital Technology, Graphic Design, CAD, Machining, Cosmetology, and Hardware & Software Maintenance.
