What is SkillForge Semester?

SkillForge Semester is a practical training programme organised by Ebonyi State University of ICT, Science and Technology, Offerekpe, Izzi. It equips students with industry-relevant skills alongside their academic studies, preparing them for entrepreneurship, innovation, and employment opportunities.

Why SkillForge?

The programme bridges the gap between classroom learning and practical experience by exposing students to hands-on training, real-world projects, and professional development.
