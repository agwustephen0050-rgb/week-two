SkillForge Training Programmes

Explore the six practical skill areas offered during the SkillForge Semester.

Find a Programme

Search or select a training area to quickly find the programme you're interested in.

Cosmetology

The Cosmetology programme equips students with practical beauty and wellness skills for professional and entrepreneurial careers.

Activities Include:

  • Hair styling and treatment
  • Makeup artistry
  • Facial and skincare techniques
  • Manicure and pedicure
  • Salon management and customer care

Hardware & Software Maintenance

Students learn how to install, repair and maintain computer hardware and software systems.

Activities Include:

  • Computer assembly
  • Operating system installation
  • Software troubleshooting
  • Hardware repairs
  • Computer maintenance

Computer-Aided Design (CAD)

Students develop technical drawing and digital design skills using CAD software.

Activities Include:

  • 2D drafting
  • 3D modelling
  • Architectural drawings
  • Engineering design
  • Project presentations

Graphic Design

Students learn creative design principles for digital and print media.

Activities Include:

  • Logo design
  • Flyer and poster design
  • Social media graphics
  • Brand identity design
  • Photo editing

Machining

This programme develops students' skills in machine operation and manufacturing processes.

Activities Include:

  • Machine operation
  • Workshop safety
  • Metal fabrication
  • Precision measurements
  • Maintenance of machining tools

Digital Technology

The Digital Technology programme prepares students for careers in the modern digital world through practical ICT training.

Activities Include:

  • HTML5 websites development
  • CSS3 web styling
  • JavaScript fundamentals
  • Cybersecurity awareness
  • Digital productivity tools
  • Team web development projects
